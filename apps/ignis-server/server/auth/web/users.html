Edit user

Disabling an account blocks sign-in and revokes its Ignis sessions. Its files and OpenID Connect link are kept. Enabling it requires a new sign-in.

Close all Ignis sessions for this user? Their account remains active. This does not sign them out of their OpenID Connect provider.

Remove this account's link to the configured provider and close all its Ignis sessions? A local password is required to retain access. If this is your account, you will be signed out.

The provider account is not deleted. Automatic account linking, if enabled, may link it again on a later OpenID Connect sign-in.
